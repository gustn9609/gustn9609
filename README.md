### About Me

I'm a **Ph.D. student** in Electrical and Computer Engineering at **The University of Texas at Austin**, co-advised by Prof. [Guandao Yang](https://www.guandaoyang.com/) and Prof. [Diana Marculescu](https://users.ece.utexas.edu/~dianam/).

My research leverages **image and video generation models** to solve challenging real-world tasks. I believe in *"Generate to Understand"* — echoing Feynman's dictum: *"What I cannot create, I do not understand."*
